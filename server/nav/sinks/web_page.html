  • Seen from above, walking up the screen.
  • Cells
    • Brighter costs more to walk through.
    • Light gray is floor outside the camera's view.
    • Dark gray is in view, but the camera didn't see floor there. Something stands in front of it or the depth has no reading.
    • The planner treats both grays as empty floor.
  • Band
    • The planned path, as wide as the body.
    • More solid the more the scene shaped it.
    • Redder the sooner something is in the way.
  • Dots are obstacles, squares are walls.
  • The two numbers
    • Scene vs Shape Metric is how far what the camera saw moved the plan away from the plan for an empty scene, in bits. An empty corridor gives 0.
    • Est. Time to Collision is how long until the body reaches the nearest thing in its path at walking speed. "nothing ahead" when the path is clear.
